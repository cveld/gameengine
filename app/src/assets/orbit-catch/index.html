Catch the falling objects!

How to play
  • Blue orb: 1 point · Green cube: 2 points · Gold gem: 5 points.
  • Catch objects before they reach the floor. Every three catches in a row raise the multiplier, up to ×4. A miss resets it.
  • AR / VR: catch with your bare hands (enable hand tracking on the headset) or with controllers. Objects fall all around you, so turn and walk to reach them. After a round, pinch or pull the trigger to play again.
  • Scoreboard: it stays where it was placed. Pinch it with thumb and index finger, or hold a controller's grip button on it, to pick it up and move it.
  • Music: techno plays during the game. Use the Music button, or click the right thumbstick in XR, to turn it on or off.
  • Browser: move the pointer or use the arrow keys.
  • Free roam does not disable the headset's safety boundary. Clear your play space and stay aware of your surroundings.
