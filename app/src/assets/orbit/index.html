Free-roam AR
Experimental unbounded tracking, falling back to local tracking if unavailable. The browser still controls passthrough and safety boundaries. Clear your walking space; keep physical space features enabled on Quest.
AR left thumbstick

Tracked hands: grab
Open your hand, bring it near the sculpture, then pinch thumb and index finger. Move your hand and release to leave the object there. Either hand works.
Tracked hands: shape
With your left index finger open and neither hand holding the sculpture, pinch left thumb and middle finger for 0.3 seconds to choose the next shape. Release before repeating.
Hand tracking
Enable hand tracking on your headset and allow the browser to use it. Controllers remain available. Tracking loss releases the sculpture; open your hand before grabbing again.
